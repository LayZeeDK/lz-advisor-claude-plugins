Run Slice A: score the verify-voter against the frozen 40-item AVeriTeC seed list, judge-free, and
publish the four confusion-matrix cells per direction with their limits.

This is the phase's cheapest certain reading and it runs FIRST among the metered ones, deliberately.
If the session pool runs dry mid-campaign, Phase 23 still has Slice A plus the Wave-3 citation dry run
plus the envelope -- which is a materially better floor under termination branch (b) than spending the
pool on the expensive uncertain capture first and getting nothing.

Purpose: Slice A is structurally immune to both root causes that killed Phases 19 through 22. It uses
no judge, so no cross-construct calibration transfer is required, and it makes no claim that needs
statistical power this n cannot supply -- it reports cells, not rates.

committed durable record.
