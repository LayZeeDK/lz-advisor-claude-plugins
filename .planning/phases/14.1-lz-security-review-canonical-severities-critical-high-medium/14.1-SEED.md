## Current state (as of insertion)

`plugins/lz-advisor/agents/security-reviewer.md` emits a fixed-order grouped
grammar (v1.0.1 milestone): `### Critical`, `### Important`, `### Suggestions`,
`### Questions`, then `### Threat Patterns` (+ optional `### Per-finding
validation`, `### Missed surfaces`). The header is the SINGLE severity source
(no inline severity token). Per-section word budgets in the
`<output_constraints>` block are regression-gated by
`D-security-reviewer-budget.sh`. The skill (`lz-security-review/SKILL.md`)
parses these headers verbatim (render-verbatim contract).

The `### Questions` section currently carries the **Class-2 `<verify_request>`
escalation hooks** and **unconfirmed hedge-marker findings** -- i.e. items
blocking a verdict pending verification, not a severity rung.

## THE open decision (resolve in discuss/research)

Critical/High/Medium is a pure severity ladder (impact x exploitability).
`Questions` and `Suggestions` are NOT severities -- they are a confidence /
recommendation axis. Migration forces a decision on both.

### Questions -- options

1. **(lean) Keep a dedicated non-severity section, renamed `### Open Questions`
   (or `### Clarifications Needed`).** 3 rungs = pure Critical/High/Medium;
   4th section explicitly documented as non-severity. Preserves the
   escalation-hook/hedge function with zero semantic loss. Canonical analog:
   pentest "Open Items / Clarifications" appendix. Lowest migration risk.
2. **Map `Questions` -> `### Informational`.** Uniform 4-rung ladder
   (OWASP/SARIF). Cleaner, but "Informational" weakens the needs-an-answer
   semantics.
3. **Drop `Questions`; fold each open item into its provisional severity via
   the existing `Unresolved hedge:` marker.** Purest 3-rung report; removes the
   dedicated escalation surface and collides with current Hedge Marker
   Discipline. Highest behavior change.

### Suggestions -- coupled decision

- Pure ladder: `Important -> High`, `Suggestions -> Medium` (inflates low-value
  hardening tips to Medium).
- Or second non-severity section `### Hardening Recommendations`, yielding
  **3 severities + 2 non-severity sections** (Hardening Recommendations + Open
  Questions) -- the shape most commercial pentest reports settle on.
